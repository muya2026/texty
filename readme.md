## 🚀 About The Game

**TEXTY** is not just another word game—it's a **futuristic cyber-puzzle experience** where vocabulary meets strategy in a neon-lit digital arena. Dive into 8 unique game modes, each designed to challenge your mind in different ways while you climb the leaderboards and unlock achievements.

### ✨ Why Play TEXTY?

- 🧠 **8 Distinct Game Modes**: From Wordle to Connections, never get bored
- ⚡ **Fast-Paced Action**: Race against time for bonus points
- 🏆 **Competitive Edge**: Global leaderboards and achievement system
- 🎨 **Stunning Design**: Immersive 3D cyberpunk aesthetics
- 📱 **Play Anywhere**: Fully responsive on desktop, tablet, and mobile

## 🕹️ How to Play

### 1️⃣ **Choose Your Arena**
Select from 8 game modes on the main menu. Each mode offers unique mechanics and scoring systems.

### 2️⃣ **Master the Mechanics**
- **Wordle**: Green = Correct position, Yellow = Wrong position
- **Boggle**: Connect adjacent letters (horizontal, vertical, diagonal)
- **Connections**: Look for subtle associations between words
- **Scrabble**: Use high-value letters (Q, Z, X) for maximum points

### 3️⃣ **Climb the Ranks**
- Earn **stars** based on speed and accuracy
- Unlock **achievements** like "Speed Demon" and "Wordsmith"
- Compete on **daily, weekly, and all-time leaderboards**

### 4️⃣ **Share Your Victory**
Beat a high score? Share it with the world! Each result screen generates a unique share card with motivational quotes from legendary thinkers.
