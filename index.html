📖 How to Play

🔤 Wordle

Guess the 5-letter word in 6 attempts. Green = correct position, Yellow = wrong position, Gray = not in word.

🪢 Hangman

Guess letters to reveal the hidden word. Don't let the hangman drawing complete!

🎲 Boggle

Find words by connecting adjacent letters in the 4x4 grid. Words must be 3+ letters.

🔍 Word Search

Find hidden words in the grid. Words can be horizontal, vertical, or diagonal.

🐝 Spelling Bee

Create words using the 7 given letters. Must include the center letter!

🔀 Unscramble

Rearrange scrambled letters to form the correct word.

🔗 Connections

Group 4 words that share a common theme or category.

📝 Quick Scrabble

Create high-scoring words from your letter tiles.
